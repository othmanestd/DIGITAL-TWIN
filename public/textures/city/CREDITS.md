# City textures: credits

All photo textures below come from Poly Haven and are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required).
Each file is the 1K diffuse map, resized and re-encoded for the city shaders. Greyscale files are detail maps:
they were converted to grey and normalised (fixed mean and contrast) so the shaders can tint them per building,
street or field. The procedural painters in `components/scenes/city/cityTex.ts` stay as the fallback.
